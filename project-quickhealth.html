Healthcare Lab Management

Diagnostics and patient-record platform with lab-device integrations and automated WhatsApp report delivery.

View Resume

Challenge

Quick Health Services required a reliable system to manage diagnostics, maintain patient records, interface with lab hardware, and seamlessly deliver test results directly to patients without manual overhead.

Solution

Developed a secure Healthcare Lab Management platform that integrates with lab devices for automated data ingestion and utilizes the WhatsApp API for instant, secure delivery of medical reports post-payment.

Architecture Highlights

  • End-to-end diagnostics and patient-record management system
  • Direct integration with physical lab devices for reliable result capture
  • Automated WhatsApp reporting triggered by payment verification workflows

Technologies

PHP, MySQL, WhatsApp API, REST Integrations

Outcome

  • Eliminated manual reporting errors and delays for patient diagnostics.
  • Increased patient satisfaction through instant WhatsApp report delivery.
  • Streamlined billing and operational workflows for lab administrators.
